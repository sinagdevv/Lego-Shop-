Every stage, numbered

Spark Voyager 900 builds in six numbered stages, each one closing with a small mechanism click so you always know a section is locked in. The cockpit module holds a warm LED puck (batteries included) that lights the interior once the canopy snaps shut. The rotating base turns a full 360° by hand, no motor, no batteries needed for that part.

Pieces arrive sorted into eight numbered bags, so you're never digging through one giant pile. The instruction booklet uses the same numbering as the bags, start to finish, stage to stage.

Wipe with a dry cloth. Keep the LED puck's battery cover closed when not in use. Avoid direct sunlight for long periods — the orange finish can fade.

Free shipping on every order, no minimum. Unopened sets can be returned within 30 days. Opened sets with all pieces accounted for qualify for store credit.
